# Allocations

The Allocations Usage dashboard summarizes how your account is consuming its
Service Unit (SU) allocation and storage.

![Allocations Usage Dashboard showing cards for SU quota, SU used, project storage and scratch storage, with a fiscal year selector at the top](<../../assets/ondemand/jobs/allocations-jobs-new-1.png>)

At the top, choose the **Fiscal Year** and confirm the **Account** you are viewing.
Four cards summarize:

- **SU Quota** — total SUs allocated for the fiscal year
- **SU Used** — SUs consumed so far, and the percent of quota
- **Project Storage** — used vs. quota
- **Scratch Storage** — used vs. quota

Below, a **Month** slider and a **QOS** selector let you scope the view. The
**SU by User** chart and the accompanying table break down SU usage per user
(UCID) for the selected period, so PIs can see who on the account is using the
allocation.

![Month slider and QOS selector used to choose the period shown on the Allocations dashboard](<../../assets/ondemand/jobs/allocations-jobs-new-2.png>)

![Bar chart of SU usage by user, with a table of usage per UCID beside it](<../../assets/ondemand/jobs/allocations-jobs-new-3.png>)


![Further SU usage charts and tables on the Allocations dashboard](<../../assets/ondemand/jobs/allocations-jobs-new-4.png>)

> the main Dashboard, or from the **Allocations Usage** button on the My Jobs page.
