# Allocations

The Allocations Usage dashboard summarizes how your account is consuming its
Service Unit (SU) allocation and storage.

![allocations-jobs-new-1.png](<../../assets/ondemand/jobs/allocations-jobs-new-1.png>)

At the top, choose the **Fiscal Year** and confirm the **Account** you are viewing.
Four cards summarize:

- **SU Quota** — total SUs allocated for the fiscal year
- **SU Used** — SUs consumed so far, and the percent of quota
- **Project Storage** — used vs. quota
- **Scratch Storage** — used vs. quota

Below, a **Month** slider and a **QOS** selector let you scope the view. The
**SU by User** chart and the accompanying table break down SU usage per user
(UCID) for the selected period, so PIs can see who on the account is using the
allocation.
